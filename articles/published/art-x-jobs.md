## Why Build Your Career at ArtX?

### 1. Modern Technology Stacks, Zero Legacy Baggage
We refuse to work on obsolete, frustrating codebases. You will work with cutting-edge tools:
- React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Start/Router, and Vite.
- Tailwind CSS and modern CSS container queries.
- Cloudflare Edge Workers, Supabase, PostgreSQL, and Redis.
- Custom, clean Gutenberg block development without bloated page builders like Elementor.

### 2. Craft-First Engineering Culture
We judge talent by output, architectural cleanliness, and problem-solving prowess—not arbitrary degree certificates. If your GitHub demonstrates clean commits, typed interfaces, and well-structured components, or your Figma displays pristine auto-layouts and design tokens, we want to speak with you.

### 3. Remote-First Flexibility with Real Work-Life Balance
We believe deep work requires uninterrupted time. We operate with minimal synchronous meetings, asynchronous documentation, and flexible hours that let you work when you are most productive.
