## 4 Actionable Steps to Dominate Hyperlocal Search in Dhaka

### 1. Optimize Your Google Business Profile (GBP) Attributes
- **Primary Category Selection:** Choose the most specific primary category available (e.g., *"Website Designer"* or *"Software Company"* rather than a generic *"Consultant"*).
- **Secondary Categories:** Add complementary categories (e.g., *"Graphic Designer"*, *"Internet Marketing Service"*).
- **Service Area Configuration:** Define specific high-value business hubs: Gulshan, Banani, Uttara, Dhanmondi, Mirpur, and Motijheel.

### 2. Implement LocalBusiness Schema Markup
Inject unambiguous microdata directly into your website's HTML `<head>`:
