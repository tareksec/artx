## Quick Decision Checklist: The 5-Point Agency Vetting Filter

> **The Short Answer:** Before hiring any web design firm in Bangladesh, verify that they pass these five non-negotiable tests:
> 1. **Live Performance Proof:** Test at least two websites from their live portfolio on Google PageSpeed Insights. If mobile scores are below 75, look elsewhere.
> 2. **Senior-Level Engineering:** Confirm that your project will be built directly by senior engineers, rather than passed down to unvetted junior interns.
> 3. **100% Intellectual Property Ownership:** Contracts must explicitly state that domain names, hosting accounts, and Git source code belong exclusively to you from day one.
> 4. **No Pirated/Nulled Templates:** Insist on custom Figma designs and bespoke, lightweight code (ACF Gutenberg or modern React) with zero pirated plugins.
> 5. **Built-In SEO & Security Auditing:** On-page technical SEO, XML sitemaps, and SSL hardening must be standard deliverables, not expensive add-ons.

## The 5-Step Evaluation Framework for Bangladeshi Businesses

### Step 1: Audit Live Portfolio Speed (Not Screenshots)
Anyone can take attractive screenshots of web layouts and upload them to a Behance portfolio. What matters is how their websites perform in the real world on a budget Android smartphone connected to a mobile network in Dhaka or Chittagong.

Ask for 3 live URLs from their recent client portfolio. Run those URLs through [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/):
- **Core Web Vitals LCP should be under 2.5 seconds.**
- **Performance Score should exceed 85 on mobile.**

If the sites take 6 seconds to load and score in the red (below 50), the agency is cutting corners with bloated templates. At ArtX, our [WordPress Development](/services/wordpress-development) and custom builds consistently hit green Core Web Vitals.

### Step 2: Clarify Technology: Custom Code vs Drag-and-Drop Bloat
Ask the agency how they intend to build your website:
- **Avoid Drag-and-Drop Builders:** Page builders like Elementor or WPBakery inject megabytes of unnecessary code and break frequently during WordPress updates.
- **Demand Custom Block Architecture:** Look for studios that build custom, lightweight Gutenberg blocks using **Advanced Custom Fields (ACF)** or modern Jamstack frameworks (React, Next.js). This ensures your site loads fast, ranks high, and remains stable for years.

### Step 3: Inspect Code and Domain Ownership Terms
Ensure your contract explicitly stipulates that:
- You own the domain name, registered under your email and mobile phone number.
- You have root administrator access to hosting, databases, and content management systems.
- You own the complete copyright to all custom design assets and source code repositories upon final invoice settlement.

### Step 4: Verify Local Payment & Delivery Integrations
If you are launching an online store in Bangladesh, your agency must have proven experience integrating:
- **bKash Merchant APIs and Nagad Checkout**
- **Payment Aggregators (SSLCommerz, Shurjopay, AmarPay)**
- **Courier API Integrations (Pathao Courier, Steadfast, RedX)**

Review our complete e-commerce delivery blueprints on our [E-Commerce Website Design Hub](/services/ecommerce-website-design).

### Step 5: Evaluate Communication & Post-Launch Support
A great agency communicates transparently, updates you via shared staging links, and provides clear project milestones. Avoid vendors who demand 100% payment upfront; standard industry practice is a **50% milestone on project initiation and 50% upon final launch and sign-off**.
