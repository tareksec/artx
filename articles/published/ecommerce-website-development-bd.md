## 6 Mandatory Features for Every Bangladeshi E-Commerce Store

### 1. Frictionless Single-Page Checkout
Bangladeshi consumers hate long 5-step checkout forms asking for postal codes and multiple addresses. We implement streamlined, single-page checkouts requesting only:
- Full Name
- 11-digit Mobile Phone Number
- Full Delivery Address
- District / Division selection (Dhaka vs Outside Dhaka delivery rates calculated automatically).

### 2. Official bKash, Nagad & Card Gateway Integration
Support both tokenized merchant checkouts and Cash on Delivery (COD). We integrate official APIs from **bKash Merchant Checkout**, **Nagad Payment Gateway**, and multi-gateway aggregators like **SSLCommerz** and **Shurjopay**.

### 3. Automated Courier API Integration (Steadfast & Pathao)
Stop copying and pasting customer names and addresses by hand. With our automated courier integration:
- Clicking "Accept Order" generates a consignment number automatically.
- Delivery labels with barcodes are printed with one click.
- Automated tracking links are dispatched to customers via SMS.

### 4. SMS OTP Verification (Fake Order Protection)
Fake Cash-on-Delivery orders and customer delivery refusal are the biggest profit killers in Bangladesh e-commerce. An automated 4-digit SMS OTP verification at checkout ensures every order is placed with a valid, reachable mobile number, reducing return rates by up to 35%.

### 5. Server-Side Facebook Conversions API (CAPI) & GA4
Due to iOS privacy restrictions and ad-blockers, standard browser tracking misses over 25% of purchase events. We implement server-side tracking, feeding accurate data directly to Meta Ads Manager for laser-targeted retargeting.

### 6. Sub-Second Mobile Performance
Over 90% of your traffic arrives via mobile phones. Our stores achieve **sub-1.5s load times** on mobile devices by compressing media to next-gen WebP formats and caching database queries in Redis.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does it take ArtX to build an e-commerce website in Bangladesh?
A standard high-performance WooCommerce store is typically completed within **10 to 14 business days**, while advanced custom headless platforms require **3 to 5 weeks**.

### How do I accept bKash and Nagad payments on my website?
You have two options:
1. **Direct Merchant Account:** Apply for official bKash/Nagad merchant accounts using your Trade License, TIN, and Bank Statement.
2. **Aggregator Payment Gateway:** Use SSLCommerz, Shurjopay, or Aamarpay, which bundle bKash, Nagad, Visa, and Mastercard into a single contract.
