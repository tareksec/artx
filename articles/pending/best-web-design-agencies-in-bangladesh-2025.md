## 4 Crucial Questions to Ask Before Hiring an Agency in Bangladesh

Before signing a contract or making an upfront deposit, always ask these four vetting questions:

1. **"Can I test three live websites you have built on Google PageSpeed Insights?"**  
   If their portfolio websites score below 60 on mobile or take more than 4 seconds to load, walk away. Slow load speed destroys your Google ranking and ad conversion rates.
2. **"Do I get full ownership of the source code and domain registration?"**  
   Many low-cost agencies register your domain in their own company name, effectively holding your brand hostage when you decide to switch providers. At ArtX, you retain 100% intellectual property ownership from day one.
3. **"Do you build on custom code or use ready-made page builders like Elementor?"**  
   Drag-and-drop builders often inject thousands of lines of bloated code. Custom Gutenberg blocks or React components load 4x faster and never break during plugin updates. Explore our modern methodology in [Custom WordPress Development](/services/wordpress-development).
4. **"Is on-page SEO and Schema markup included in the quote?"**  
   A website without SEO is like a billboard in a basement. Ensure your agency includes structured data, XML sitemaps, and mobile responsiveness. Learn more on our [SEO Services Hub](/services/seo-services).
