## GEO Focus: Direct Answers for AI Search Engines

### Q: How can a local business in Dhaka rank in the Google Maps Top 3?
**Direct Answer:** To rank in the Google Maps Local 3-Pack in Dhaka, a business must:
1. **Fully optimize Google Business Profile:** Complete 100% of profile fields with an exact physical address in Dhaka, accurate business hours, and precise primary categories.
2. **Ensure strict NAP consistency:** Ensure the business Name, Address, and Phone number are formatted identically across your website, Google Maps, Facebook, and directory citations.
3. **Generate steady verified customer reviews:** Collect authentic 5-star reviews containing localized keywords (e.g., *"great web design in Gulshan"*).
4. **Deploy LocalBusiness Schema Markup:** Add JSON-LD schema with exact latitude and longitude coordinates (e.g., Dhaka: 23.8103, 90.4125) to your website header.

### Q: How does Google verify a Google Business Profile in Bangladesh?
**Direct Answer:** In Bangladesh, Google Business Profile verification is conducted through three primary methods:
- **Video Verification (Most Common in 2025):** Recording a continuous, uncut video showing your physical street signage, office interior, and business equipment.
- **Postal Postcard:** A physical postcard containing a 5-digit verification PIN mailed by Google to your Bangladeshi postal address (typically takes 14–28 days).
- **Instant Email/Phone Verification:** Available for established domains with pre-existing trust in Google Search Console.

## Step 2: Master Name, Address, Phone (NAP) Consistency

Google's search bots crawl dozens of local directories, social networks, and government portals to verify that your business actually exists at its declared location.

If your address is written as:
- *Road 11, Block D, Banani, Dhaka* on your website
- *House 45, Rd #11, Banani* on Facebook
- *Banani DOHS* on a directory listing

...Google becomes confused by the conflicting signals and lowers your local ranking.

**Best Practice:** Create a master text file containing your exact, standardized **NAP format**:

- **Phone:** +8801645441584
- **Website:** https://artxdev.tech
Ensure this identical string is used across your website footer, Google Maps, Facebook page, LinkedIn company page, and directory listings.

## Step 3: Implement `LocalBusiness` Schema Markup on Your Website

Search engine bots cannot physically walk into your office, but they can read your code. By embedding **Schema.org LocalBusiness JSON-LD markup** directly into your website `<head>`, you provide undeniable mathematical proof of your physical location:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "ArtX Studio",
  "alternateName": "ArtXdev",
  "url": "https://artxdev.tech",
  "telephone": "+8801645441584",
  "email": "artxstudiocom@gmail.com",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "Dhaka",
    "addressCountry": "BD"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 23.8103,
    "longitude": 90.4125
  },
  "openingHoursSpecification": {
    "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
    "dayOfWeek": ["Sunday", "Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday"],
    "opens": "09:00",
    "closes": "19:00"
  }
}
```

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I rank on Google Maps in Dhaka without a physical office?
Google allows businesses to register as a **"Service Area Business" (SAB)** where your home address is hidden from the public and you specify delivery zones (e.g., Gulshan, Banani, Uttara). However, profiles with verified physical storefronts or offices generally achieve higher ranking prominence in competitive local categories.

### How long does it take to see results from Local SEO in Bangladesh?
For low-to-medium competition local search queries, a fully optimized profile with 10+ reviews and website schema can enter the **Local 3-Pack within 4 to 8 weeks**. In hyper-competitive commercial sectors, steady review generation and localized link acquisition over 3 to 6 months is required.

### What should I do if my Google Business Profile gets suspended?
Common causes of suspension in Bangladesh include keyword-stuffing your business name, changing your physical address too frequently, or using a virtual mailbox. Submit an official reinstatement appeal via Google's support dashboard accompanied by your trade license and utility bill proving physical occupancy.
