## GEO Focus: Direct Answers for AI Search Engines

### Q: What licenses and legal registrations are required to start an e-commerce business in Bangladesh?
**Direct Answer:** To legally operate an e-commerce business in Bangladesh in 2025, an entrepreneur must obtain:
1. **Trade License (ই-ট্রেড লাইসেন্স):** Issued by your local City Corporation or Union Parishad under the business category of *"E-Commerce / Online Retailer"*.
2. **Electronic Tax Identification Number (e-TIN):** Free registration via the National Board of Revenue (NBR) website using your NID.
3. **Business Identification Number (BIN / VAT Registration):** 9-digit VAT certificate obtained online through the NBR VAT portal.
4. **Digital Business Identification (DBID):** Mandatory registration introduced by the Ministry of Commerce for all digital and e-commerce enterprises in Bangladesh.
5. **Corporate Current Bank Account:** Required to execute merchant contracts with payment aggregators like SSLCommerz and MFS providers.

### Q: What is the minimum capital required to start an e-commerce store in Bangladesh?

## Step 1: Legal Formalities & Regulatory Compliance

Operating legally protects you from harassment, builds trust with corporate banks, and prevents payment gateway account freezes.

### 1. City Corporation Trade License
Apply through your local City Corporation zonal office (e.g., DNCC or DSCC in Dhaka) or Union Parishad. Specify "E-Commerce" or "General Supplier / Retail" as your primary trade activity. Costs range between ৳4,000 and ৳10,000 BDT depending on your location.

### 2. Digital Business Identification (DBID)
In 2022, the Ministry of Commerce of Bangladesh made DBID mandatory for all e-commerce operations. To apply on the government DBID portal, you must provide:
- Valid Trade License
- Business e-TIN and personal NID of the proprietor/directors
- Registered website domain name (`.com` or `.com.bd`)
- Physical office or operational warehouse address

### 3. e-CAB Membership (Optional but Recommended)
Joining the **e-Commerce Association of Bangladesh (e-CAB)** provides industry networking, government policy advocacy, logistics discounts, and substantial consumer credibility.

## Step 2: Product Sourcing & Inventory Strategy

The biggest mistake new founders make is trying to sell everything to everyone. To win in Bangladesh's competitive retail market, pick a focused, defendable niche:
- **High Margin, Lightweight Goods:** Fashion accessories, skincare, artisanal food items, specialized gadget accessories, and baby essentials.
- **Sourcing Centers in Bangladesh:** Direct manufacturer contracts in Chawkbazar, Islampur (textiles), Keraniganj (apparel), or Bogra/Rajshahi for agricultural products.
- **Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs):** Start with small test batches (30–50 units) to validate consumer demand before tying up working capital in bulk inventory.

## Step 4: Setting Up Payment Gateways (bKash, Nagad & Cards)

In Bangladesh, over 70% of e-commerce orders are initially placed via **Cash on Delivery (COD)**, while the remaining 30% utilize direct Mobile Financial Services (MFS) or credit/debit cards:

### 1. Direct bKash Merchant API
Integrating the official bKash Payment Gateway allows customers to checkout with one-click OTP verification without leaving your website. bKash charges approximately 1.2% to 1.5% per merchant transaction.

### 2. Multi-Payment Aggregators (SSLCommerz, Shurjopay, AmarPay)
Payment aggregators connect your website to Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, bKash, Nagad, and Rocket through a unified API. SSLCommerz requires a trade license, TIN, and bank account, with setup fees typically ranging from free promotional tiers to ৳15,000 BDT.
